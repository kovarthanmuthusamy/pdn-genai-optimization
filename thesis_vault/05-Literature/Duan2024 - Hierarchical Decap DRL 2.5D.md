# Hierarchical Decoupling Capacitor Optimization for Power Distribution Network of 2.5D ICs with Co-Analysis of Frequency and Time Domains Based on Deep Reinforcement Learning

**Authors:** Yuanyuan Duan, Haiyang Feng, Zhiping Yu, Hanming Wu, Leilai Shao, Xiaolei Zhu  
**Year:** 2024 · **Venue:** arXiv:2407.04737 (rev. 2025)  
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04737  
**Tier:** Supporting — cite in passing

## Why it matters here

Frequency-domain impedance optimization followed by time-domain refinement on a voltage violation integral. Relevant as the argument that frequency-domain feasibility alone is an incomplete acceptance criterion — a limitation your surrogate shares, since you never leave the frequency domain. Note the authors themselves flag the experiments as preliminary, so cite it for the framing, not for numbers.
