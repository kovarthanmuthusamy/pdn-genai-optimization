# Surrogate-Assisted Framework for SI-Compliant Interconnect Design Optimization Using the Earth Mover's Distance

**Authors:** Emre Ecik, Werner John, Julian Withöft, Ralf Brüning, Jürgen Götze  
**Year:** 2026 · **Venue:** arXiv:2606.15234 (Jun 2026)  
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15234  
**Tier:** Supporting — cite in passing

## Why it matters here

Ranks candidate designs by EMD distance to an ideal reference waveform, with a decision-tree 'quality gate', deliberately avoiding inverse modelling and stochastic search. A direct alternative to your selection rule (feasible, then lowest peak impedance) — worth contrasting, since EMD scores whole-shape similarity whereas your rule is a hard mask test plus a scalar.

## Where it goes in the thesis

Ch.6 selection rule justification; Ch.8 metric choice.
