# Multimodal Variational Autoencoders for Semi-Supervised Learning: In Defense of Product-of-Experts

**Authors:** Svetlana Kutuzova, Oswin Krause, Douglas McCloskey, Mads Nielsen, Christian Igel  
**Year:** 2021 · **Venue:** arXiv:2101.07240  
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.07240  
**Tier:** Supporting — cite in passing

## Why it matters here

Empirical PoE-vs-MoE comparison concluding PoE suits *conjunctive* modality combination, MoE *disjunctive*. This is the defence of your architectural choice: occupancy, spectrum and heatmap are conjunctive views of one physical state, so PoE is the principled pick — an argument you should make explicitly rather than leaving PoE unmotivated.
