**Authors:** Azalia Mirhoseini, Anna Goldie et al.  
**Year:** 2021 · **Venue:** Nature 594

## Why it matters here

The highest-profile demonstration that learned models can do EDA placement, and the reason reviewers will expect the comparison. Also a cautionary case: it drew sustained reproducibility criticism, which supports your insistence on ground-truth re-simulation before any design claim.
