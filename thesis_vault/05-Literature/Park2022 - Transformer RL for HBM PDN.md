# Transformer Network-based Reinforcement Learning Method for Power Distribution Network (PDN) Optimization of High Bandwidth Memory (HBM)

**Authors:** Hyunwook Park, Minsu Kim, Seongguk Kim, Joungho Kim et al. (KAIST)  
**Year:** 2022 · **Venue:** arXiv / IEEE TMTT  
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15722  
**Tier:** CORE — belongs in the literature review

## Why it matters here

Attention-based RL that parameterizes the decap-optimization policy directly and optimizes self- and transfer impedance at multiple ports. Reports minutes versus >1 month for full search — the same amortization argument you make, reached by a different route. Useful for justifying why a learned model is worth the training cost at all.

## Where it goes in the thesis

Ch.2 related work; Ch.1 motivation for amortizing the simulate-inspect-adjust loop.
